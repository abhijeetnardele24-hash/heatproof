# HeatProof Video Demonstration & Walk-Through Script

> **Target Duration**: Exactly 3 Minutes (180 Seconds)  
> **Audience**: IEEE ClimateChain Hackathon Judges, Municipal Resilience Directors, Loss & Damage Funders  
> **Presenter Tone**: Calm, authoritative, technical, civic-minded  

---

### [0:00 - 0:30] The Deadly Heat Problem & The Adaptation Verification Gap
- **Visual**: Open on HeatProof landing page on a laptop screen (`docs/assets/screenshots/01_civic_wayfinding_desktop.png`). Ambient temperature and heat index telemetry visible in the live hazard banner.
- **Narrator**:
  > *"Across the Global South, extreme heatwaves are turning cities into deadly convection ovens. When ambient heat indices exceed 42°C, access to clean drinking water, misting canopies, and emergency ORS cooling stations is a matter of life and death.*  
  > *Yet cities face an enormous blindspot: municipal maps show cooling centers that are padlocked, water dispensers that ran dry hours ago, and unverified infrastructure. Meanwhile, climate adaptation funds cannot verify if physical services were actually delivered.*  
  > *This is HeatProof: the verifiable proof-of-adaptation network for extreme-heat relief infrastructure."*

### [0:30 - 1:00] Live Meteorological Hazard Data & Mineral Civic Wayfinding
- **Visual**: Switch zones from Pune Central to Ahmedabad. Point out the live telemetry card updating: Heat Index 45.1°C, Extreme Danger band. Click on interactive SVG thermal map pins. Filter by "Drinking Water" and "Cooling Center".
- **Narrator**:
  > *"HeatProof connects directly to live Numerical Weather Prediction data via the Open-Meteo REST API, calculating apparent heat stress in real time using the NOAA Steadman and Rothfusz heat index equations.*  
  > *Our mineral civic UI is fully responsive across mobile and desktop, localized in English, Hindi, and Marathi, and designed to WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility standards.*  
  > *Citizens and gig workers can instantly see verified operational cooling facilities nearby, with clear proof badges and witness corroboration counts."*

### [1:00 - 1:40] Privacy Boundary & Responsible Multimodal AI Reporting
- **Visual**: Switch to Mobile View (`docs/assets/screenshots/02_mobile_reporting_ai.png`). Click "Report Update" on Mandai Hub. Highlight the green Privacy Boundary alert. Select the "Clean Flow" photo. Watch the AI proposal evaluate.
- **Narrator**:
  > *"When a field volunteer reports a facility’s status, HeatProof enforces a zero-PII privacy boundary. GPS coordinates are coarsened client-side to a 1.1-kilometer grid, EXIF camera metadata is stripped, and personal phone numbers or names are regex-redacted before anything leaves the device.*  
  > *Under our 'AI Proposes; Humans Decide' governance model, our multimodal AI evaluates physical visual evidence—distinguishing clean flowing water from broken taps or padlocked gates. But the AI cannot touch the ledger. The citizen reviews the proposal, confirms observations, and signs."*

---

### [1:40 - 2:15] Independent Witness Attestation & The 9-State Invariant Engine
- **Visual**: Close modal. Click "Corroborate Attestation" on a nearby facility. Enter witness verification notes and click "Record Attestation on Ledger". Toast confirms on-chain anchoring.
- **Narrator**:
  > *"A single report never changes a critical state. HeatProof's formal 9-state invariant engine requires N ≥ 2 independent witness attestations to verify an asset as operational.*  
  > *However, if ambient heat index surges past 42°C into the Danger threshold, our heat escalation protocol automatically lowers outage quorum to single-witness reporting, instantly prioritizing emergency municipal water tanker dispatch."*
